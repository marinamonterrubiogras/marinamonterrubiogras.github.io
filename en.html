Coming soon

If an item arrives late or sells out with a restock planned, it stays live as coming soon: the wait turns into demand.

  1. 1

    Late or sold out

    Not in stock in time for the drop, or sold out with a restock planned.

  2. 2

    Coming soon

    Live with a waitlist: it builds a queue and demand.

  3. 3

    Back in stock

    On the day it arrives, it goes live on the site.

  4. 4

    The flow sends the alert

    Klaviyo alerts the whole list: a flow with good conversion.

Strategy

Drop launches

Every drop has its own cycle, adjusted to the number of items. This is a four-week cycle.

  1. 1Week 1

    Planning

    Items and delivery times from Design and Buying. Products start being created in Shopify.

  2. 2Week 2

    Briefing & shoot

    The e-commerce briefing goes to Communications for the shoot and to the other teams.

  3. 3Week 3

    E-commerce build

    Photos on the site, homepage and New In, content for paid and newsletters, and pre-launch.

  4. 4Week 4

    Drop & analysis

    Launch on the site, newsletter and paid, with a follow-up two days later. Glovo and the stores receive the information. Analysis for the next drop.
